Profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Geotechnical Engineering at Concordia University, working at the intersection of cold regions geotechnics, frozen ground mechanics, and geothermal energy systems. My research investigates how freeze-thaw cycles reshape subsurface stress, heat, and fluid flow — processes with critical consequences for infrastructure in permafrost regions. I develop coupled thermal-hydraulic-mechanical (THM) finite-element tools and physics-informed neural networks to simulate permafrost dynamics and geothermal heat-exchanger operations in cold climates.
