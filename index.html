Profile

I am a PhD Candidate in Civil Engineering at Concordia University and recipient of the FRQ Doctoral Research Scholarship, specializing in Geotechnical and Geothermal Engineering and multiphysics modeling of porous media.

My research focuses on the coupled thermal, hydraulic, and mechanical behavior of porous media, with applications to geothermal energy systems, freeze-thaw processes, and climate-resilient infrastructure in cold regions. Through numerical modeling and open-source finite element frameworks, I investigate subsurface evolution under natural and engineered thermal disturbances. My work aims to support the development of sustainable geothermal technologies and improve the resilience of infrastructure under changing climatic conditions.
