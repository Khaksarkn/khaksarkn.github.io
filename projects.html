Projects

MOOSE-Based TH/THM Freeze-Thaw Simulator

Developed the group's first coupled thermal-hydraulic-mechanical (THM) application using the MOOSE framework to simulate freeze-thaw processes in cold region soils, including phase-change, frost heave, and pore pressure evolution.

MOOSE C++ FEM THM

Standing Column Well Geothermal Modeling

Modeled standing column well (SCW) geothermal systems at field scale and validated simulations against thermal response test (TRT) data from Varennes, QC. Investigated permafrost preservation and talik formation under heat-exchanger operations.

Geothermal Field Scale Building Heating

ERT-Constrained Frozen Ground Models

Integrated electrical resistivity tomography (ERT) derived subsurface data with borehole temperature observations to initialize spatially heterogeneous frozen ground models for permafrost site characterization.

ERT Borehole Temperature Heterogeneity Permafrost

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Multiphysics Freeze-Thaw

Developing PINN frameworks to accelerate freeze-thaw multiphysics simulations, embedding governing PDEs (heat conduction, Darcy flow, poromechanics) as physics constraints in the neural network loss function.

PINN Python Machine Learning
